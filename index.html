The quiet side of the Lowcountry, with a tee sheet.

Callawassie is a gated island community near Okatie, between Bluffton and Beaufort and under an hour from Hilton Head, reached by a causeway over the marsh. Mornings & Mulligans is a golf-included vacation rental that sits right on the course.

Mornings start in the sunroom, coffee in hand, watching herons work the pond and the first groups come through the 9th tee. Afternoons split off: a round on the Fazio course, a swim at one of the two club pools, a bike ride under live oaks and Spanish moss. Evenings end back on the deck as the light goes gold over the water.

It's built for golf trips with friends, families visiting a new Marine at Parris Island, and anyone who wants a few slow days somewhere beautiful. The kitchen is stocked, the beds are good, and the TV is optional — one guest told us they never turned it on all week.

Questions, answered.

Is golf really included?
Yes. Every stay includes 2 complimentary rounds of championship golf per day for two golfers, worth about $250 a day. The course is closed Mondays. Club rentals are available at the pro shop.
Is this a standalone house?
It's a townhouse: one side of a well-insulated duplex with its own entrance, deck and driveway. You'll have the entire home except the garage and one locked owner's closet.
How does pricing work for 5 or 6 guests?
The nightly rate covers up to 4 guests. For 5 or 6, there's an extra $20 per guest per night, added automatically at booking.
What's the minimum age and are pets allowed?
The booking guest must be at least 25. Pets, smoking and events aren't permitted. Kids and infants are welcome.
Where is Mornings & Mulligans, and how far is Hilton Head?
It's on Callawassie Island, a gated community near Okatie, SC, between Bluffton and Beaufort. Bluffton and Parris Island are about 30 minutes away, downtown Beaufort is under 30, Hilton Head Island and Savannah are under an hour, and Charleston is under 90 minutes.
How many people does it sleep?
Up to 6 guests across three bedrooms: a primary suite with a king bed, a queen room and a twin room, plus 2 full bathrooms.
How does check-in work?
Before arrival you'll complete a short online check-in (rental agreement and ID) through our Happy Guest portal — no app required. Your door code arrives the day before check-in, and your stay includes damage protection in place of a traditional security deposit.
