A short drive to Parris Island

Parris Island is about 30 minutes from the island, close enough for family day and graduation week and far enough to feel like a real getaway. One recent guest wrote that it was “a short drive to Parris Island, which made it so convenient for our visit.” Always confirm event dates and base access rules with official Parris Island sources before you travel.

Space for the whole family

The townhouse sleeps up to 6: a king primary suite, a queen room and a twin room, with 2 full bathrooms, a stocked kitchen and a back deck overlooking the pond. Kids and infants are welcome, and the booking guest must be at least 25.

Something to do between events

Stays include 2 complimentary rounds of golf per day (closed Mondays), and the island has pools, tennis and pickleball, free bikes and a fitness center. Downtown Beaufort is under 30 minutes away.
