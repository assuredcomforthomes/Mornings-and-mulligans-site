Close to downtown Beaufort

Historic downtown, the waterfront park and shrimp boats on the Beaufort River are under 30 minutes away. Callawassie itself is a gated island community reached by a causeway over the marsh, so you get quiet evenings after a day out.

Bluffton's Old Town galleries and oyster bars are about 30 minutes in the other direction, and Parris Island is about 30 minutes away.

Golf and the island

Two complimentary rounds per day at Callawassie Island Club's Tom Fazio-designed course are included for two golfers (closed Mondays). The island also has pools, tennis and pickleball, free bikes and a fitness center.

The house

Up to 6 guests, three bedrooms (king, queen and twin), 2 full baths, a stocked kitchen and a back deck overlooking the pond and the 9th tee.
